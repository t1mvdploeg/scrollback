# Give motion one job at a time

## What is explained
- The example reveals the headline first, then the image, then the short introduction.
- The timeline shown uses a 180ms fade and a small 12px translation.
- A second version respects reduced motion by showing the content immediately.

## Tools, repos and links mentioned
- GSAP: The import and a short timeline are visible in the editor. (on screen)

## Design
- Colours: Warm paper #F5F1E9, dark ink #232721 and muted blue #567AAB; light mode.
- Type: A large serif headline with plain sans captions and body text.
- Layout: An editorial hero, broad margins and a narrow text column below one image.
- Effects: 180ms fades, 12px movement and a static reduced-motion alternative.
- Looks like: Editorial magazine
- Libraries: GSAP (on screen)

## Core claim
Motion works best when it clarifies the order of the content.

## Usefulness
The timing and reduced-motion example make the idea easy to judge. The short timeline is visible, but it has not been run or tested here. It is a useful reference for one small transition; it does not justify animating every section.
