# Style: the designs you saved

[← Overview](OVERVIEW.md)

9 posts show a website, app or interface.

## Recurring style

- **Colours:** A restrained neutral base with muted blue, olive or terracotta accents. (9 posts)
- **Type:** A plain sans for supporting text; expressive serif headlines on the editorial pages. (9 posts)
- **Layout:** A clear content hierarchy, aligned edges and space between groups. (9 posts)
- **Effects:** Short, quiet fades instead of continuous decorative motion. (5 posts)

## Style groups

9 of the 9 posts fall into a group.

### Quiet editorial (6 posts)

Warm paper, dark ink, expressive serif headlines and broad margins. Fine rules give the page structure; the story remains the main event.

- **Posts:** [Quietform Studio](items/room-for-the-story.md), [Paperplane Practice](items/a-smaller-type-system.md), [Morrow Type Lab](items/the-secret-layout-kit.md), [Softgrid Collective](items/let-the-grid-do-the-work.md), [Stillpath Motion](items/motion-with-a-purpose.md), [Northfold Portfolio](items/show-the-decision.md)
- **Take from it:** Use one strong heading, a comfortable reading measure and enough empty space to make the hierarchy clear.

### Focused dark tools (3 posts)

Deep blue-gray panels, compact sans type, mono shortcut hints and grouped rows. Centered dialogs keep actions close to the content.

- **Posts:** [Small Signal Workshop](items/a-command-menu-that-listens.md), [Orbitkit Lab](items/the-everything-starter.md), [Tidyloop Office](items/an-inbox-you-can-empty.md)
- **Take from it:** Keep the app shell quiet; make focus, selection and the next action unmistakable.
